
The basis_*.png images are en emergency solution to provide front pages in different languages. These images have been created as follows:

- The English covers have been used as starting point
- First of all AI was used to remove the English text from the covers. This was done in OpenCode using an image plugin using GPT Image 2.5 as the model.
- The AI did good work, but there was loss of resolution an fidelty.
- As a result the photo at the front cover has been pasted pack using Gimp.
- This means the most visible part of the front cover is pixel identical to the original.
- The MEGA65 logo in the header was recreated in SVG, using potrace and manually improved in Inkscape.
- The M-E-G-A logo in the footer  was recreated in SVG, again by AI, in OpenCode using the free Space Bunny model.
- The text below the logo in the footer was identified as the PixelSplitter font and manually added in Inkscape.
- The translated titles of the books are added in SVG using the MegaGlacial font.

While the result is quite good, this approach is not optimal I hope the basis_*.png images can be recreated using the original software that was used to create the English covers. It does have advantages: The MEGA65 logo, M-E-G-A logo and text below and the title of the book are now creating using SVG primitives and will thus be rendered at print resolution, i.e. there will be as sharp as they can be.

Further, by moving the book titles to SVG, they can be edited in-repository, which aligns better with the spirit of open-source and will be useful for future translations.

By removing the logos & title from the .png files they have become easier to compress, resulting in somewhat smaller PDF files.
